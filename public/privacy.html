Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

QuieTube (https://quietube.exitcode0.net/) is a free, browser-based tool operated by Tom Cocking, United Kingdom ("we", "us"). It lets you sign in with your Google account, see your YouTube subscriptions sorted by each channel's most recent upload, and unsubscribe from channels you no longer want to follow. This policy explains what data QuieTube accesses, how it is used, and how you can control it.

1. QuieTube uses YouTube API Services

QuieTube uses YouTube API Services to read your subscriptions and channel upload information and, when you ask it to, to remove a subscription. By using QuieTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What data QuieTube accesses

When you sign in with Google, QuieTube requests the following permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — to list the channels you are subscribed to, to look up each channel's uploads playlist and most recent upload date, and to delete a subscription when you click "Unsubscribe". QuieTube never creates, uploads, rates, comments on, or otherwise modifies any YouTube content.
  • profile — your name and profile picture, shown only so you can confirm which account is signed in.

Through those permissions QuieTube processes the following Authorized Data:

  • Your subscription list: subscription IDs, channel IDs, channel titles and thumbnail URLs.
  • For each subscribed channel, the ID of its uploads playlist and the publish date of its most recent video.
  • Your Google account display name and profile picture URL.

3. How data is used

Authorized Data is used solely to display your subscriptions sorted by activity and to carry out unsubscribe actions you explicitly request. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose other than providing the QuieTube feature you are using.

4. Where data is stored, and for how long

QuieTube has no backend server and no database. All API calls are made directly from your browser to Google's servers. Specifically:

  • Your Google access token is held only in your browser's memory while the page is open. It is never written to disk and is discarded when you close the tab or sign out.
  • To save API quota on repeat visits, the processed subscription list (channel names, thumbnails and last-upload dates) is cached in your browser's localStorage for a maximum of 24 hours, after which it is automatically deleted. The cache is also deleted immediately when you click "Sign out" or "Refresh".
  • We never transmit, copy, or store any Authorized Data on servers we control, and we never share it with third parties.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

QuieTube uses Google Analytics to measure aggregate, anonymous page usage (for example, how many people visit the site) and displays advertisements served by Google AdSense to help cover running costs. These Google services may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's advertising policies. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

No YouTube Authorized Data is ever passed to Analytics or AdSense, and ads are not targeted using any information obtained through YouTube API Services.

6. Revoking QuieTube's access

You can withdraw QuieTube's access to your Google account at any time:

  • Click Sign out in QuieTube — this revokes the current access token and clears the local cache; or
  • Remove QuieTube from your Google Account's connected apps at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

To clear any remaining cached data, clear your browser's site data for quietube.exitcode0.net.

7. Children

QuieTube is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact
